# Mastering Terraform within Azure landscape

Navigating Azure through portal.azure.com has become almost second nature to me. Knowing exactly what to look for and where to find it was my initial goal when beginning my Azure journey. That goal has now evolved to being able to deploy and configure resources as granularly as I do in the Azure portal, but through IaC (Infrustructure-as-Code). IaC weapon of choice? Terraform.

## Learning Pathway

We're going to get our hands dirty pretty quickly on this journey considering how lean the the terraform syntax in general is. What we'll be spending most of our time on is the Azure-specific syntax, `azurerm_resource_group`, `azurerm_virtual_network`, etc. That way we can quickly collect small wins and begin compounding on our learnings.
